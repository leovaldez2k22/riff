| Q18-004 | A1 step 5 | Riff Preview 7f7ab8e | Low | Some clearly held words do not glow or grow. Example: ABBA, "Dancing Queen", the line "Dancing queen" at 1:36: "queen" is held but stays plain. Timing from BetterLyrics' TTML for the song (fetched from the computer, 15 Oct 2026): Dan 1:36.443-1:36.859, cing -1:37.273, queen 1:37.273-1:38.406, so "Dancing" 830 ms (119 ms per letter) and "queen" 1,133 ms (227 ms per letter) | Confirmed by arithmetic against LyricsModels.kt heldLyricWords: a word is held at 1,600 ms or more, or at 900 ms or more when its time per letter is at least 1.5 x the line's median. | Q18-005 | A2 step 4 (owner's report) | Riff Preview 7f7ab8e | Medium | Seeking sometimes stops the sound for too long before it carries on, on streamed **and downloaded** songs; worst when seeking **back** to an earlier part | During playback, drag the seek bar ahead, and back to an earlier part; sometimes playback halts, then resumes after a delay | Owner's report, 18 Oct 2026; no recording or diagnostics yet | Not investigated; read from the code only. Both ExoPlayers (MusicPlaybackService.kt, AudioTransitionCoordinator.kt) use Media3's default load control, which keeps no back buffer, so a seek back in a streamed song discards and downloads again what was already played; a seek ahead past the buffer needs a new range request (possibly a fresh stream URL). A downloaded song is a local file, so a gap there points elsewhere: candidates are a file with no seek index (e.g. WebM/Matroska without Cues, so a seek has to scan the file), the audio pipeline (booster processor, PCM-only output) being flushed and rebuilt, or the main player seeking while crossfade's tail player is prepared. Measure first: time from seek to sound for a streamed and a downloaded song, forward and back, with buffering events from the diagnostics; check the downloaded file's container for a seek index. Fix candidates: a back buffer (setBackBuffer) for streams, cues or a seek index on download, and whatever the measurement shows for local files | Open |

| Q18-010 | A6 step 2 | Riff Preview 7f7ab8e | **High** (account data isolation) | After a Change account that did not finish (Q18-009), Riff still shows and uses the old account (the owner's), but Google's own account page inside Riff's sign-in browser lists the owner's account as **Signed out** and the new (dummy) account as the signed-in one. Riff's committed account and the cookies it reads from have come apart | Settings > profile > Change account; sign in to another Google account until Q18-009 stops it; Back to Riff; then Change account again and look at Google's "Choose an account" list | artifacts/quality/q18a/a6/a6-05-account-after.png (Riff: "Connected to YouTube Music", the owner's name and picture) and a6-06-accounts-page.png (Google's list: the owner's account signed out, the dummy account signed in; local only, shows e-mail addresses) | Confirmed in the code, effect not yet observed: ProviderSessionManager reads the session cookies from WebView's shared CookieManager, the same store the sign-in browser writes to. refreshAccount() (every app start) and persistRotatedCookies() (whenever YouTube rotates a cookie) load whatever is in that store into the live session, while dataSyncId and authUser stay the old account's.
