## Downloads, search and storage

- **Delete all downloads** replaces the settings shortcut on Downloads. It asks for confirmation, removes the current account's audio/artwork/lyrics bundles, and cancels pending transfers.
- **Download all songs** is available on playlist and album pages once all continuation pages have loaded. It uses the complete eligible collection regardless of filtering or sorting, deduplicates repeated songs, and retains completed downloads.
- Search has a **cross icon** to clear the entire query.
- The download budget retains 1–16 GB choices and adds **24, 32, 64, 128 and 256 GB**, then **Unlimited**. Unlimited removes the app budget cap; device free-space reserves still apply.
- **Clear playback cache** asks for confirmation before clearing. Permanent downloads are retained.

## Lyrics, glass and queue navigation

- Lyrics timing defaults and resets to **+0.2 seconds**.
- Lyrics timing, player three-dot menus, song long-press menus, sort menus and language menus use frosted backgrounds with sharp text and controls.
- **Settings → Appearance → Frosted glass intensity** controls the frost separately from **Glass intensity**. High-contrast controls use opaque backgrounds.
- The translation metrics sentence is removed from the lyrics page. On-device translation behavior is described in [privacy and permissions](privacy.md).
- Tapping the current-song thumbnail while viewing the queue returns to the expanded player through the artwork transition.

## Automatic crossfade

Enable **Settings → Audio → Crossfade** and choose a duration from 1–12 seconds. An eligible automatic queue handoff blends the outgoing and incoming audio with equal-power gains. Audio items remain eligible when their optional video URI is absent. Live/video playback, repeat-one and active sleep timers do not blend; pauses, seeks and disabling crossfade cancel the blend.

Artwork, title and artist in the full player, mini player and queue header follow the audio engine's live crossfade progress for its entire duration. Manual song selection/skip and crossfade-off playback replace content immediately. Reduced motion remains respected.

Both gains follow a bounded monotonic clock. Incoming startup and buffering hold progress instead of compressing the fade, delayed updates cannot cause a sudden gain jump, and an early outgoing end cannot abruptly restore incoming volume. The outgoing renderer attaches the same loudness processing as the main player once its audio session is available; volume-boost changes update both.

Ordinary online audio uses direct transport, with cache identities separated by transport, format and bitrate. This addresses the earlier short playback interruption while keeping the selected audio format and bitrate. Downloads, live and video retain their transport capabilities.
